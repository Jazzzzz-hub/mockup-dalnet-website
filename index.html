ABOUT DALNET

One network. Many ways to participate.

Started in 1994 as an alternative to the overburdened networks of its time, DALnet grew into a vibrant and friendly IRC community. It pioneered nickname and channel registration, giving users practical tools to protect their identity and their space online.

Over the years, DALnet has placed a strong emphasis on user wellbeing, documentation and accessible support. From services and help channels to coders, staff and volunteer teams, the network continues to be sustained by people who care about IRC as a living form of online communication.

DALnet's future depends on those volunteers and on the users who still value direct conversation, open communities and the culture that grew around Internet Relay Chat.
